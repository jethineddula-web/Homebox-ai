Which AI chat site should you use with Homebox?

{{articleMeta}}

Homebox works with many AI chat sites, including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Studio, Arena, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen, Mistral, Kimi, Perplexity, Copilot, Poe, HuggingChat and Z.ai. Models change every few months, so instead of crowning a winner, this guide explains what actually matters for Homebox-style work and how to compare sites yourself in a few minutes.

What matters for working with Homebox

1. Following instructions precisely

Homebox gives the AI a short manual: write actions in ```homebox blocks, use these tools, stop and wait for results. Models that follow formats carefully finish tasks with fewer hiccups. If a site often answers with plain code instead of homebox blocks, it will need more reminders.

2. Working in many rounds

A real task can take 10 to 30 rounds of acting and reading results. Some sites handle long conversations better than others. A model that stays on track over many rounds will finish bigger tasks.

3. Knowledge of your tools

For code, most popular models are strong. For 3D and creative apps, check how well the model knows the specific API: maya.cmds, Blender's bpy, Houdini's hou or ExtendScript. A quick test (below) shows this fast.

4. Limits and plans

Free plans often limit messages per hour or day. Because Homebox sends results back automatically, a long task uses many messages. If you hit limits often, a paid plan on your favourite site or splitting work into smaller tasks helps.

5. The site's terms

Each AI service has its own terms, and some don't allow their websites to be used through automated tools. Read the terms of a site before using Homebox with it. AI apps with MCP support, such as Claude Desktop, are an officially supported way to connect tools like Homebox. See the FAQ.

6. Privacy

Everything the AI reads through Homebox is sent to that AI's company. Check each site's data settings, such as whether chats may be used for training, and choose what you're comfortable with.

Compare:

For your own field, swap in a small task from your real work, such as a simple Maya rig or an Excel summary.

Special cases

  • Arena: use Direct Chat. Battle mode shows two answers side by side, so Homebox asks you to pick one before running anything.
  • Sites not in the list: open the site and click Enable Homebox here in the extension popup. If the site's design is unusual, paste mode on the Connect AI page always works.
  • Desktop AI apps: apps that support MCP, such as Claude Desktop, Cursor or VS Code, can use Homebox's tools directly through its private MCP link.

The short answer

Pick a site whose terms allow this kind of use, test it with the task above, and switch only if it struggles with your kind of work. Homebox works the same way on all of them, so switching costs nothing.
