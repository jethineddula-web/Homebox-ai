About Homebox

Homebox is made by Jethin Production, an independent studio that builds tools for artists, designers and developers.

Why we built it

AI chat sites have become remarkably good at writing code, scripts and documents. But getting that work onto your computer still means copying from the chat, pasting into files or apps, running it, copying errors back and doing it all again. For a 3D artist asking for a rig in Maya, or a small business owner asking for a spreadsheet, that loop is the slowest part.

Homebox closes the loop. The AI you already use can create files and work in your apps directly, while you keep the final say over every change.

What we believe

  • You stay in control. Clear limits by default, visible approvals, one-click undo and an emergency pause.
  • Your computer stays yours. No accounts, no cloud relay and no tracking inside the app. Homebox only talks to your own PC.
  • Use any AI. Homebox isn't tied to one AI company. Use whichever chat site works best for you today.
  • Plain language. Every setting explains itself, and the app guides you step by step.

Who it's for

People who want AI to do real work, not just talk about it: 3D artists and technical directors in Maya, Blender and Houdini; designers in Photoshop and Illustrator; streamers in OBS; office workers in Excel and Word; and developers who want quick prototypes.

Who makes Homebox

Jethin, founder of Jethin Production

Jethin designs and builds Homebox, and writes the guides on this site. The guides come from building Homebox and working with the apps they describe, and they're updated when Homebox or the apps change. If something in a guide doesn't match what you see, please let us know and we'll fix it.

How we write our guides

  • Accurate steps. Every guide follows what Homebox actually does and uses each app's real menu names. If something looks different on your computer, tell us and we'll update the guide.
  • Honest limits. We say what doesn't work, such as apps that can't undo script changes, as clearly as what does.
  • Kept current. Each guide shows when it was published and last updated.
  • Independent. We aren't paid by, or affiliated with, the AI companies or software makers we mention.
