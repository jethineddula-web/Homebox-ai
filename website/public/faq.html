Frequently asked questions

General

What is Homebox?

Homebox is a free Windows app and browser extension. It lets AI chat websites such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini work in one folder on your computer, and in apps like Maya, Blender, Photoshop and Excel. You decide what the AI may do and approve its steps.

Does Homebox cost anything?

Homebox is free. You use your own account with whichever AI service you choose, under that service's terms.

Which AI chat sites work?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Studio, Arena (LMArena), DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen, Mistral Le Chat, Kimi, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Poe, HuggingChat and Z.ai work out of the box. For any other site, open it and click Enable Homebox here in the extension popup.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Homebox 2.0 is made for Windows 10 and 11, and the Adobe and Office connections use Windows automation.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera, or any browser that can load Chrome extensions.

Using Homebox

Why does the AI give me code instead of doing the work?

The task was typed into the chat directly. Start a new chat and use the round Homebox button → Start task. That sends the AI its instructions along with your task.

What is Auto-run?

It's the switch called Let the AI keep working by itself in the Homebox panel on the chat page. When it's on (the default), Homebox runs the AI's steps as soon as it finishes writing them, sends the results back and continues until the AI says DONE. Turn it off in the chat-page panel if you'd rather press Run yourself.

What is Full access?

A switch in the Homebox window that stops the approval prompts. Everything you have turned on runs without asking; things set to Off stay off. A banner shows while it's on, and one click turns it off. Pause still stops everything.

Can I undo what the AI did?

Yes. Homebox backs up every file before changing it; click Undo on the Activity page. In most 3D apps, Word and AutoCAD, each AI step is also a normal undo step. Excel and PowerPoint cannot undo script changes, so save first.

Why start a new chat after changing settings?

The AI learns which tools it may use at the start of a task. A new chat gives it the updated list.

How many steps can the AI take?

Auto-run pauses after 30 rounds by default so it can't loop forever. Change the round limit in Settings (turn on Show advanced options first), or turn the switch on again to continue.

Am I allowed to use Homebox with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other AI sites?

That depends on each site's terms, which you agreed to when you signed up, and which can change. Some AI services don't allow their websites to be used through automated tools, or only allow automation through their official programming interfaces (APIs) or apps. Homebox types your task into the site and sends the AI's results back for you, which some sites may count as automation.

Before you use Homebox with a site, read that site's terms. If they don't allow it, don't use Homebox with that site. Breaking a site's terms can get your account limited or closed. AI apps that support MCP, such as Claude Desktop, offer an officially supported way to give an AI tools like Homebox's. See our terms, section 4.

Can Homebox fill in job applications, LinkedIn or emails for me?

Homebox can read your CV (PDF or Word) and help with applications on websites you allow: the AI reads the form, fills in what it can and writes a cover letter, and you approve each step. It never presses the final Submit or Apply button, never types passwords or card details, and never sends emails by itself: emails open as a draft for you to check and send.

Homebox does not control LinkedIn, because LinkedIn's User Agreement doesn't allow tools that automate its website. Instead, the AI writes your profile text and gives you Copy buttons to paste it in yourself. Other job sites have their own terms, so check them before using the AI there. Many sites don't allow mass automated applications.

Privacy and safety

Does Homebox send my files anywhere?

Homebox itself sends nothing to us and has no account. Files the AI reads are sent to the AI site you're using, because that's how the AI sees them. Keep private documents out of the sandbox folder.

Can other people on my network use my Homebox?

No. Homebox only accepts connections from your own computer (127.0.0.1), and browsers must be approved with a code.

Is Full access dangerous?

It can be, because the AI acts without asking. Commands and app scripts can reach outside the sandbox folder. Use it for tasks you trust, keep commands off if you don't need them, and use Pause if anything looks wrong.

Apps and advanced use

Which apps can the AI control?

Maya, Blender, Houdini, 3ds Max, Cinema 4D, Nuke, Unreal Engine, Substance 3D Painter, Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, AutoCAD, OBS Studio and VS Code. See Supported apps.

Can the AI see my screen or viewport?

No. The AI only sees text results, such as object names, sizes and positions. Describe what looks wrong and it will adjust.

What is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol is a standard way for AI apps to use tools. Claude Desktop, Cursor, VS Code and others can connect to Homebox's private MCP link without the browser extension.
