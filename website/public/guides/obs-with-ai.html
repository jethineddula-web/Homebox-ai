Control OBS Studio with AI

By Jethin · 30 September 2026 · 2 min read

OBS Studio has a built-in remote control called obs-websocket. Homebox uses it to let an AI send OBS requests, such as "switch to the Gameplay scene" or "start recording", with your approval.

1. Turn on the WebSocket server in OBS

  1. In OBS, open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings.
  2. Tick Enable WebSocket server. Leave the port at 4455 unless you changed it.
  3. Keep Enable Authentication on and click Show Connect Info to see the password.

2. Connect Homebox

  1. In Homebox, open Apps → OBS Studio and turn it on.
  2. Paste the password into the box, check the port and click Save. Homebox tests the connection straight away.
  3. Start a new chat so the AI learns about the OBS tool.

3. Things to ask

  • "List my OBS scenes and sources." (uses GetSceneList and GetInputList)
  • "Switch to the Just Chatting scene." (SetCurrentProgramScene)
  • "Mute the Mic/Aux input." (SetInputMute)
  • "Start recording, and stop it in the next step when I say so." (StartRecord / StopRecord)
  • "Create a new scene called Break with a text source that says Back soon."

Each request appears in Homebox with its data for approval. Results come back as JSON, so the AI can see scene names and states.

Requests can change what you stream or record. Keep approvals on while you're live, and use Full access only for setup work off-stream.

Troubleshooting

  • "OBS is not reachable": OBS is closed or the WebSocket server is off.
  • "OBS rejected the password": copy it again from Show Connect Info. It changes if you click Generate Password.
  • "OBS error 600" or similar: the scene or input name doesn't exist. Ask the AI to list scenes first.

Example: a "be right back" routine

Once OBS is connected, one sentence can replace several clicks:

In OBS: switch to the "Be right back" scene, mute Mic/Aux,
and pause the recording. When I say "back", do the reverse.

The AI turns this into a short series of requests: SetCurrentProgramScene, SetInputMute and PauseRecord. Each appears in Homebox for approval, with its exact data.

Useful request names

RequestWhat it does
GetSceneListLists your scenes and shows which one is live.
SetCurrentProgramSceneSwitches the live scene.
GetInputListLists your sources (inputs), such as microphones and cameras.
SetInputMuteMutes or unmutes a source.
StartRecord / StopRecord / PauseRecordControls recording.
StartStream / StopStreamControls streaming. Approve these with care.
SetSceneItemEnabledShows or hides a source inside a scene.

The AI knows these names, but you can ask it to "list scenes and sources first" so it uses your exact names.

Staying safe while you are live

OBS requests take effect immediately, and some can't be taken back: once a stream has started, viewers have seen it. A few habits help:

  • Keep approvals on while live. Full access is fine for building scenes before a stream, but not during one.
  • Prepare scenes in advance. Let the AI build and name your scenes while you are offline. While live, only switch between them.
  • Read stream and record requests twice. StartStream and StopStream are the ones that matter most.
  • Use Pause in an emergency. One click in Homebox stops all AI access, without touching OBS itself.
