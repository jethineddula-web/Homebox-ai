Let AI work on your computer, with you in control

Homebox connects the AI chat sites you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Arena, DeepSeek and more) to one folder on your PC and to apps like Maya, Blender, Photoshop and Excel. The AI does the work. You stay in control.

One folder, nothing else

The AI can only see the sandbox folder you choose. Secret files like .env and SSH keys stay hidden, even inside it.

You approve changes

Reading files can be automatic. Changing files, running commands and controlling apps waits for your OK, unless you turn on Full access.

↩️

Undo file changes

Homebox saves a copy before every file change, and one click on the Activity page puts it back. In most 3D apps, each AI step is one Ctrl+Z.

How it works

  1. Install Homebox and the browser extensionHomebox runs quietly on your PC. The extension adds a round Homebox button to AI chat sites.
  2. Give the AI a taskOpen a new chat, click the Homebox button, type what you want, for example "build a to-do app in index.html", and press Start task.
  3. Watch it workThe AI writes small action steps. Homebox runs them, sends the results back and keeps going until the AI says DONE. You approve each change or let Full access handle it.

Why people use Homebox

No copy and paste

Without Homebox, you copy code from the chat, paste it into files, run it, copy the errors back and repeat. Homebox does that loop for you, step by step, and shows you exactly what it did.

Works with the AI you choose

Use Homebox with AI chat websites in your browser, or with AI apps that support MCP, such as Claude Desktop. You use your own account, under that service's terms. Check the terms first.

Built for creative work

Ask the AI to block out a scene in Maya or Blender, build a node network in Houdini, lay out slides in PowerPoint or switch scenes in OBS, and watch it happen live.

Everything stays on your PC

Homebox only listens on your own computer (127.0.0.1). It has no cloud account and sends nothing to us. The only thing that leaves your PC is what the AI site you chose already sees.

Ready to try it?

Setup takes about five minutes. The live guide inside Homebox walks you through every step.
