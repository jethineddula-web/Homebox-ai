Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information Jethin Production ("we", "us") collects through this website (https://www.example.com) and through the Homebox app, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. The Homebox app

The Homebox app runs on your own computer. It does not have user accounts and does not send your files, settings, activity or usage data to us. Settings and the activity log are stored locally in the .homebox folder in your user folder, and you can delete them at any time.

When you use Homebox with an AI chat website or an AI app, the content the AI reads or creates is exchanged with that AI provider under their own privacy policy. We don't receive or control that data. Please review the privacy policy of each AI service you use.

2. Information this website collects

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to improve Homebox.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit, to keep the site secure and working.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. This site and our advertising partners may use cookies, as described below and in our cookie policy.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

This website may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy settings link shown on the site.

4. How we use information

We use information to run and secure this website, answer your messages, understand which pages are useful, show advertising that helps keep Homebox free, and meet legal obligations.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising. To make a request, email hello@example.com.

6. Children

This website and Homebox are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and security

We keep emails only as long as needed to help you and for our records. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as AI chat services and software vendors. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@example.com.
