What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They help sites work, remember choices and, for advertising, show more relevant ads and measure them.

Cookies on this website

TypeWho sets themWhy
EssentialOur hosting providerSecurity and load balancing. The site can't work without them.
ConsentOur consent tool (Google)Remember your privacy choices.
AdvertisingGoogle AdSense and its partnersShow ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and, with your consent where required, personalize them.

The Homebox app itself doesn't use cookies to track you. The control panel on your computer stores your display preferences (such as theme) in your own browser only.

Your choices

  • Change your consent at any time using the privacy settings link on this site (shown to visitors in regions where consent is required).
  • Turn off personalized ads from Google at Google Ads Settings, or from many vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. Some parts of sites may not work without them.
